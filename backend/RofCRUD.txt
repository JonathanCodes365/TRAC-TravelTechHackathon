It means: Pydantic is allowed to get data from an object's attribute?
Ok why do we need that and what is this cryptic meaning ?

attributes like : report.id , report,type, report.message, report.location right ?

So our pydantic expects data more like in the form of a dictionary...

So, essentially we are saying Pydantic is allowed to get data from attributes of ReportModel.
